## The ordinary day

The baseline is small and repeatable: show up for a little while, most days,
and be in your Chinese, whatever that comes to mean for a given learner.
Consistent steps, easy to rejoin. A missed day leaves the next day the same
gesture.

The richness of language study lives inside that while. Different ways of
practicing, material that is personal, a structure that fits this person: that
is the scenery of the walk. Choosing among ways to learn sits outside it.
That choice is where overwhelm starts, and where a heroic sprint starts. A
sprint is a mood that is hard to repeat tomorrow. The ordinary day is meant
to be repeatable on a flat one.

Intentional wandering names the quality of attention. The learner is actually
in the language, the while has some character, and the while is not organized
around clearing a metric. Over weeks the trace should feel like a life in the
language: I have been around my Chinese. A single while is not graded.

The user-facing frame is the while itself. A time budget, a due count, and the
question of whether these minutes were well spent all pull the learner up into
supervising the practice. Internally the system still cares that the while goes
somewhere. That care stays off the surface.

"My Chinese" is the aftertaste of fit. It shows up when practice feels useful
and intuitive enough that the learner does not audit it. The system makes the
space. The ownership is what that space feels like from inside.

## The inner loop

Most of a learner's attention, on the order of four fifths to nine tenths,
stays in the inner loop: try, find out, go on. The fraction describes attention
across a week. It is not a quota of screens inside one while.

The next thing is obvious enough that choosing never becomes the activity. One
recognizable way of arriving, and one rhythm, so the body knows how to be here.
The interior changes: a distinction, something from the week, a stretch a bit
past you, a few minutes that keep a new binding warm.

A shift in what the practice is asking feels like another street on the same
walk. When a new kind of practice resets the room — new instructions, a new
sense of which activity this is — the learner pops out. Retainment that the
system quietly considers necessary shares the same gait. Felt as a scheduled
segment, it becomes a chore, and the outer loop starts.

Usefulness, from inside, is absorption at your edge: not bored, not lost. A
reason why this moment is high value is already a small ejection.

Leaving is standing up. No duration question on the way in, and no verdict on
the way out. The while ends because the learner leaves, and that ending is
clean.

A standing risk: a walk that only follows what already feels pleasant circles
the same block, and it feels like yours the whole time. The system's quiet care
is to keep the neighborhood from shrinking. The learner meets the result later,
still from inside, as more of their Chinese actually being usable, including
things they used to go around. A recommendation to work on an area would turn
that care into a briefing.

## The outer loop

The remaining attention is a real surface. The learner knows when they have
come up. Two manners, with different weights.

**A tap on the glass.** Always findable, almost never used. When something
snags, the learner can mark a bearing without a long departure: more like this,
this isn't mine, stay with this. The while continues. Nothing interviews them
afterward. While they are under, the tap stays quiet. A loud tap makes rating
a second activity, and the outer loop moves into the practice.

**A weekly breath.** A different room, explicitly for looking around. It shows
the week as what the learner was in, in a form they can recognize as theirs.
It asks for bearings:

- Did this feel like yours?
- Anything you wanted to be in that you weren't?
- Anything you were glad to leave alone?

Glad-to-leave-alone treats an untouched thing as a possible wish.

The room can offer a lean. A lot of the week had one texture; keep wandering,
or lean the other way for a few days and see how it feels. A lean is a probe.
The learner goes back under, the next whiles have a slightly different grain,
and nobody asks midweek whether it is working. The next breath is soon enough
to say it held, or that it washed out, or to say nothing.

Saying nothing is in good standing. The whiles are still good when a learner
never comes up. The quiet care continues either way. If the weekly room were
required for the practice to know where it is going, skipping it would become
a second pile.

The tone of the room stays small. A gap, a neglected region, or a suggested
focus invites a Monday sprint in some people. A heading change is a few days
long and easy to wash out.

The system's reasons stay out of the room. The echo of a lean is the texture
of the following week, nameable once in ordinary language the next time the
learner surfaces: more of this week was you trying to get things said.

Both affordances speak in the learner's bearings: saying things, reading, this
bit of my life, stay with this, not mine. Named in exercise types and
priorities, reorienting asks the learner to design the walk, and the menu
reappears on Sunday.

A prompted check-in during the week is rare. It earns a place when the
practice has lost the learner's bearings. A pulse question while things feel
fine teaches people to live at the surface.

Wording of the tap, the weekly questions, and the lean is illustrative. The
rhythm and the altitude are the orientation.

## What later ideation is for

The next work is the inner loop, in scenes of staying under: arriving, a
middle where the kind of practice changes without a reset, leaving, a day that
is easy to rejoin, a snag and the tap. A scene succeeds when it keeps the
learner in their Chinese. A scene that is efficient per minute and ejects them
into evaluating the minute has missed this orientation.

The outer-loop rooms above are enough of a picture to protect while that
ideation happens. Still open: the exact wording, how often a breath really
wants to happen, and how much invisible steering a learner can absorb before
fit starts to feel like being managed.
