## Staged-flow cue ownership

This is the diagnosis stage of a staged reflection. Every newly drafted word-owned production cue must accept exactly its owning word. Do not put alternate words in a new cue's acceptedWordIds. A shared semantic-axis elicitation is considered by a separate promotion stage; diagnose that need with the existing tags and explain it, but do not fabricate selectivity or a multi-answer word-owned cue.
