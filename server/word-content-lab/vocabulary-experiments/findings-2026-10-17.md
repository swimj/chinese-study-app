# Vocabulary authoring: first editorial trials

This note records the first independently reviewed prompt trials for the new
vocabulary practice experience. The strongest next review candidates are general
v4 and teaching v2. The work preserves the existing prompts' emphasis on felt,
graspable meaning, lexical integrity and natural expression while allowing a
larger repertoire. These candidates have not been adopted into production by this
experiment chat.

## Evidence and limits

The trials used `gpt-5.6-luna` with high reasoning and a 12,000-output-token bound.
Twenty-one completed calls produced 163 draft exercises. All completed outputs
passed structural validation. One initial sandbox-blocked request is retained as
failed, with unknown usage/cost. Completed-call cost estimates total $0.0421 using
the repository's retained price basis; billing was not audited.

There was one sample per word/variant, not repeated-seed measurement. Three words
were compared throughout: 报备, 泡沫 and 不堪. General v3 also tried 藤椒 and
为所欲为. These are familiar source fixtures, some already present in the older
prompts' examples, not a held-out learner corpus. The reviews are Codex editorial
judgments, not learner feedback or retention evidence.

The compact comparator preserves the existing practice prompt's editorial policy,
with its input/output shape adapted to the experiment harness. It is not a replay
of the old production serving path.

## What changed in the outputs

| Word | Compact baseline | Broad v1 | Broad v2 | Broad v3 | Broad v4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 报备 | 2 | 14 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| 泡沫 | 2 | 16 | 9 | 9 | 12 |
| 不堪 | 2 | 16 | 9 | 7 | 10 |

Counts describe volume, not scores. The different pools can all contain useful
material; a higher count is not a winner by itself.

**Broadness needs to retain easy entry points.** V1 mostly expanded scenarios and
framed clozes. For 泡沫 it produced full sentences such as “After I shook the drink,
foam rose to the top,” but lost the compact concept anchor. V2 explicitly retained
simple cues where they fit, and “Foam or lather.” returned alongside other routes.
V4 kept that direction and added context without making every item a story.
The useful change was opening options while preserving plain retrieval.

**Answer scope matters more than a universal preference for definitions or
clozes.** V1 and v2 gave 不堪 direct cues such as “Utterly exhausted; completely
worn out.” Those naturally ask for the whole state, while the target supplies
only part of constructions such as 疲惫不堪. A hidden note about intensification
does not repair the visible exercise. V3's softer warning still left whole-state
direct cues in the output. V4 makes scope the first decision and permits an
all-cloze pool for uses that need surrounding construction. It produced, for
example:

- English: “Utterly exhausted after working all day.”
- Mandarin: `忙了一整天，我已经疲惫____了。`
- Target: 不堪.

That is a better candidate for this contribution. It does not imply that ordinary
nouns or verbs should be forced into clozes: v4 preserved direct meanings for
报备 and 泡沫.

**Delivered teaching is a useful generation boundary.** The 报备 test supplied
both formal and relationship uses in the source but only the first four formal
introduction beats as delivered teaching. Both teaching variants stayed with
formal notification. V2 returned a plain meaning and the familiar property-management
cloze. For 不堪, v1 mixed grammar-definition drills and constructions; v2 returned
three clozes practising the introduction's exhaustion, noise and weak-team examples.
That feels like a coherent, compact teaching repertoire without changing the
introduction itself.

**Exploration still produces material to reject or revise.** One v1 泡沫 cue called
cappuccino foam “lather,” an awkward English choice. A v3 为所欲为 cue asked for a
“four-character expression,” adding an artificial form clue. Some extended 不堪
collocations in v4 merit manual review. For example, I would revise the generated
`现场的惨状令人____入目。` rather than accept its naturalness from a schema check.
The [Ministry of Education entry](https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=2065)
provides a narrower vulgar/low-quality usage for 不堪入目 and a straightforward
predicate example. That does not settle every modern usage or prove the generated
sentence ungrammatical; it is an additional reason to review its scope and wording.

## Suggestions

Use general v4 and teaching v2 as the next review starting points. Preserve their
core editorial model while shortening wording later only if that does not lose
it. Avoid universal direct/cloze quotas: simple practice for a constructional use
can itself be a familiar short cloze.

Keep generous pools and near variants eligible for trial. The current outputs
mostly vary familiar gloss/circumstance/cloze routes; they do not yet demonstrate
a radically different exercise repertoire or that small changes help learners.
Do not discard plausible variation for failing to prove that benefit in advance.

For constructional targets, review expansion into additional idioms/collocations
with particular care. A use ID can be valid while the phrasing is awkward. More
contexts within sound constructions can be valuable without extending the taught
sense inventory or multiplying learner obligations.

Make the next human review concrete: inspect the v4 不堪 pool beside v2/v3's direct
cues, then the v4 泡沫 pool to check that the scope rule did not over-constrain an
ordinary noun. Finally, inspect teaching v2 beside its actual introduction.
